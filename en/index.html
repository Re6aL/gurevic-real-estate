About the agency

Ilia Gurevich — founder and agent of GUREVIĆ

We are in our third year on the Montenegrin property market. The agency now works in a new format: buyers, tenants, investors and owners each have a separate direction with its own terms and process.

Agreements with clients are made in writing. For legal matters, design and construction we work with specialist partners.

More about the agency
Process

How we work

Four stages, from your first message to the deal.

  1. Request

    You describe the task: what you are looking for or which property you offer, your timing and budget.

  2. Shortlist or valuation

    We shortlist suitable options or assess your property and agree on the terms of our work.

  3. Viewings and negotiation

    We arrange viewings and negotiate price and terms with the other party.

  4. Deal under contract

    The terms are set out in a written agreement in line with Montenegrin law.
