    Buying property in Montenegro with GUREVIĆ

    The catalogue lists apartments, houses, villas and plots for private construction on the coast and in other parts of Montenegro. Hotels, commercial property, development land and construction projects are in the investment section.

    Before the purchase we help you check the property: request the list nepokretnosti — the land registry extract showing the owner and any encumbrances — agree the terms with the seller and prepare the deal for signing before a notary.

    Temporary residence

    For third-country nationals not covered by the statutory exceptions, property with an official tax valuation of €150,000 or more may serve as grounds for temporary residence, provided the other legal requirements are met. We check the conditions as of the application date — this website is not a substitute for legal advice.
