About the agency

A real estate agency in Budva

GUREVIĆ (DOO GUREVIC BUDVA) is a real estate agency in Budva, run by its owner and agent Ilia Gurevich. You discuss the price, the terms and the progress of the sale directly with the person who handles your deal.

We are in our third year on the Montenegrin property market and now work in a new format: separate services for buyers, tenants, investors and owners, written agreements, and partners for legal support, design and construction.

On the seller's side we also take on investment assets — development land, construction projects, hotels, restaurants and other operating businesses. Details of such assets are shared with buyers confidentially.

Our process

How we work with owners

Five steps from the first conversation to the deal.

  1. 1

    Valuation

    We view the property, review the documents and the market, and agree the asking price with you.

  2. 2

    Photography

    We photograph the property and prepare the listing description.

  3. 3

    Agreement

    The terms are set out in writing:

    • agency commission of 5% excl. VAT, other terms by agreement;
    • an exclusive agreement is preferred;
    • any change of price only with the owner's consent;
    • the owner provides access to the property for viewings.
  4. 4

    Marketing and viewings

    We publish the property, answer enquiries, hold viewings on an agreed schedule and report the results to you.

  5. 5

    The deal

    We pass on offers, support you in negotiations and see the deal through to signing.

We work under a written agreement in accordance with the laws of Montenegro.

Why an exclusive agreement

It is not mandatory, but it is more convenient for the owner.

  • One point of contact instead of meeting every agent and agency in Montenegro.
  • The property is on the market with one price and one description.
  • Viewings follow a single agreed schedule.

Property sale agreement — main sections

Sample for information only — the final text is provided at signing.

  1. PartiesThe owner — an individual or a company — and the agency DOO GUREVIC BUDVA; details and representatives of the parties.
  2. The property and its identificationType and size of the property, title deed number (list nepokretnosti), cadastral municipality (KO) and, for an apartment, the unit number (PD).
  3. Price and price changesThe asking price; it may only be changed with the owner's consent.
  4. Agency fee5% excl. VAT; when payment becomes due and how it is settled.
  5. Exclusivity and termExclusive or non-exclusive mandate, term of the agreement and how it is extended.
  6. Owner's obligationsProvide the property documents, give access for viewings, disclose encumbrances, tenants and other circumstances relevant to the sale.
  7. Agency's obligationsValuation, photography, marketing, viewings, reporting to the owner, negotiation support and assistance until the contract is signed.
  8. ConfidentialityHandling of the owner's documents and personal data; which property details are published.
  9. TerminationGrounds and procedure for early termination of the agreement.
  10. Governing lawThe agreement is governed by the laws of Montenegro.

Business sale agreement — main sections

Sample for information only — the final text is provided at signing.

  1. PartiesThe business owner or selling company and the agency DOO GUREVIC BUDVA; details and representatives of the parties.
  2. Subject of the saleCompany details (name, registration number) and what exactly is sold — a share in the company or the business's property and assets.
  3. Price and price changesThe asking price and what it includes; it may only be changed with the owner's consent.
  4. Agency fee5% excl. VAT; when payment becomes due and how it is settled.
  5. Exclusivity and termExclusive or non-exclusive mandate, term of the agreement and how it is extended.
  6. Owner's obligationsProvide company documents and information for the buyer's checks, give access to the premises for viewings, disclose liabilities and encumbrances.
  7. Agency's obligationsValuation, preparing the description, finding buyers, viewings, reporting to the owner, negotiation support and assistance until the contract is signed.
  8. ConfidentialityBusiness details are disclosed only to qualified buyers and, where needed, after an NDA is signed.
  9. TerminationGrounds and procedure for early termination of the agreement.
  10. Governing lawThe agreement is governed by the laws of Montenegro.
