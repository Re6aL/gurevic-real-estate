Property management

What can be arranged

There is no fixed package: we agree on the tasks you actually need.

  • Key holding

    The agency keeps the keys — for viewings, tradespeople and emergencies.

  • Utilities and bills

    Checking and paying utility bills in an agreed way.

  • Property checks

    Inspections at check-in and check-out of a long-term tenant and, by arrangement, during the tenancy.

  • Maintenance and repairs

    Coordination of contractors; the cost of works is agreed with the owner in advance.

Terms

  • Terms are agreed individually.
  • Tasks and reporting are agreed in advance.
  • Arrangements are put in writing.

Frequently asked questions

What does management include?

The tasks are agreed individually: for example, key holding, paying utility bills, property checks during a long-term let and coordinating repairs through contractors.

How much does it cost?

Terms are discussed individually and depend on the property and the tasks.

Can management be combined with letting?

Yes, we can discuss it: for example, finding a long-term tenant and staying in touch with them afterwards.

Do you carry out repairs yourselves?

No, we coordinate contractors. The cost of works is agreed with the owner in advance.
