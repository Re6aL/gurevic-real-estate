Due diligence

What we analyse

Before recommending an asset, we check the key parameters. In-depth review is carried out by specialists.

  • Ownership and encumbrances

    Title deed (list nepokretnosti), history of rights, mortgages, seizures and other restrictions.

  • Urban-planning conditions and permits

    Permitted use, building parameters, existence and status of permits.

  • Technical condition

    Structure and building services, and whether the property matches its documents.

  • Financials

    Revenue and costs as declared by the seller — and their verification against documents.

  • Market comparables

    Asking and transaction prices for comparable assets in the area.

  • Exit options

    Resale, leasing, change of use — which scenarios are realistic for the asset.
