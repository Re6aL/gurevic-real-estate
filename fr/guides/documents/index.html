Documents et légalisation de propriété

Vérifie les appartements, les maisons et les terrains avant un dépôt ou un contrat d'achat.

Révisé: 05.11.2026

Documents and property legalisation

Request a current list nepokretnosti and check ownership, encumbrances and the registered property. For buildings, check construction documents, use permission and legalisation status. For land, check permitted use, planning conditions, access and applicable UTU.

Why a title extract is not enough

The legalisation law includes restrictions on transferring unauthorised buildings and specific exceptions. A missing permit needs a property-specific review. Plot subdivision and development potential must be verified with legal, cadastral and licensed planning professionals.

Sources

  • Zakon o legalizaciji bespravnih objekata — 20.07.2026

Informations générales uniquement. Un notaire, un avocat et un conseiller fiscal doivent vérifier comment les règles s'appliquent à votre transaction.
